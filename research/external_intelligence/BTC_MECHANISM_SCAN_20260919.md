## Mechanisms retained for future preregistration

### 1. Intraday momentum / reversal conditioned by market state
Source: Wen, Bouri, Xu, Zhao, *Intraday Return Predictability in the Cryptocurrency Markets: Momentum, Reversal, or Both* (SSRN, 2022).
Mechanism: BTC intraday predictability can alternate between momentum and reversal; reported dependence on large intraday jumps, FOMC announcements, liquidity and COVID regime.
Use: candidate for a later BTC intraday horizon cell, only after timestamp-clean intraday data, explicit cost/slippage model and low-DOF preregistration.
Risk: high turnover and regime-conditioning researcher degrees of freedom.

### 2. Cross-sectional crypto momentum
Sources: Liu, Tsyvinski, Wu, *Common Risk Factors in Cryptocurrency*; Drogen, Hoffstein, Otte, *Cross-sectional Momentum in Cryptocurrency Markets* (2023); Zhang & Makgolo, *Cross-Sectional Dispersion and the State Dependence of Cryptocurrency Momentum* (2026).
Mechanism: relative-strength/momentum across a liquid crypto universe; newer evidence suggests cross-sectional dispersion may condition later momentum strength.
Use: NOT a single-BTC standalone signal; candidate for later BTC-centered crypto-universe / relative-selection layer after BTC v1 core is stabilized.
Risk: survivorship bias, universe reconstruction, delistings, liquidity screens, exchange availability and multiple testing.

### 3. Cross-sectional reversal anchored to formation-period lows
Source: Nakagawa & Sakemoto, *New behaviorally-based cross-sectional reversal portfolios in the cryptocurrency market and market uncertainty* (2025 revision).
Mechanism: behavioral anchoring/reversal portfolio using formation-period lows; reported robustness across formation horizons and conservative costs.
Use: later independent cross-sectional family, not a rescue of rejected simple BTC daily mean-reversion.
Risk: requires point-in-time multi-asset universe; not comparable to single-asset Z/RSI mean-reversion failures.

### 4. Cointegration / relative-value portfolios
Source: Gabriel & Kunst, *Cointegrated Portfolios and Volatility modeling in the Cryptocurrency Market* (2024).
Mechanism: cointegrated crypto baskets/spreads with volatility modeling.
Use: later market-neutral/relative-value family.
Risk: structural breaks, unstable cointegration, borrow/funding/execution realism.

### 5. Volatility-state conditioning — adverse corroboration for V1 interpretation
Source: Tripathi, *Regime-Dependent Volatility Sensitivity* (2026).
Mechanism/evidence: reports failure to reject equivalence of Bitcoin trend performance across volatility states and argues volatility sensitivity is asset-dependent rather than a universal defensive filter.
Governance interpretation: this is NOT used to retrofit V1. It independently supports retaining the frozen V1-V1 adverse/qualifying result without threshold rescue. V1 remains non-promoted as standalone Alpha/risk-control component unless a separately authorized new lineage is opened for a distinct economic mechanism.

Use: candidate only for a separately frozen intraday/session family; current Cycle-2 daily calendar controls remain rejected/not promoted and are not reopened.
